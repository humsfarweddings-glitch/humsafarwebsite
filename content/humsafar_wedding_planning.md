personalized roadmap from the engagement to the final farewell —

Whether you're planning a
**destination wedding in India** or an

## Venue Selection & Booking – Finding Your   Perfect Place to Say “I Do”

Whether you envision a royal wedding in a palace, a beachfront pheras at

sunset, or a quiet ceremony in the hills, we help you find the venue that

mirrors your vision and values. With access to the most sought-after venues

across India and international destinations, we ensure your wedding has the

perfect setting — one that feels just right for your story. From scouting,

comparisons, virtual walkthroughs (for NRI clients), and negotiations to final

booking — we handle it all.

**_Because the right place doesn’t just look beautiful it_**

**_feels like home for your heart._**

## Budget Management & Vendor Coordination – Creating Value Without Compromising Vision

We believe in transparent, stress-free planning — starting with a realistic budget aligned to your priorities.

From there, we recommend and negotiate with trusted, quality vendors — from décor and catering to photography and transport — ensuring the best value without compromising on quality or aesthetics.

**_You dream, we align the right people and resources to make it real — beautifully and responsibly._**

![dimensions](https://humsafar-wedding.netlify.app/images/wedding-planning/dimensions.jpg)

## Event Scheduling & Timeline Creation – Where Every Moment Falls into Place

Every wedding has its own rhythm. We build a detailed master timeline for each function — factoring in rituals, guest movements, vendors, rehearsals, and buffer time — so everything flows smoothly and stress-free.

From mehendi mornings to midnight pheras, we keep everyone — including families, artists, and teams — perfectly aligned.

**_Because the best weddings feel effortless… and that’s_**

**_our planning, working silently behind the scenes._**

**_Your guests are your world. We treat them like family —_**

**_because their joy is your joy._**

**_Seamless execution means peace of mind — for you,_**

**_and for everyone you love._**
