# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## From India to the World — Humsafar Wedding FAQs for You

### About Humsafar Weddings

**What is Humsafar Weddings, and what services do you offer?**
Humsafar Weddings is a premium wedding planning company that specializes in luxury destination weddings across India and abroad. We offer end-to-end services including wedding planning, theme décor, logistics, guest hospitality, entertainment, and complete on-ground execution.

**Do you only plan destination weddings?**
Not at all. While destination weddings are our forte, we also curate luxury weddings in your hometown or any location of your choice with the same passion and precision.

**Why choose Humsafar Weddings over other wedding planners?**
We focus on emotionally personalized, story-driven celebrations with world-class execution. From concept to coordination, our attention to detail, design innovation, and smooth management set us apart.

### Destination Wedding Planning in India & Abroad

**What are the best locations for a destination wedding in India?**
We specialize in India's top wedding destinations such as:
- Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur – for royal palace weddings
- Goa and Kerala – for beach and tropical vibes
- Jim Corbett, Mussoorie, and Rishikesh – for forest and hill station experiences

**Can you plan our wedding abroad?**
Yes! We plan weddings internationally in locations like:
- Bali, Thailand, Dubai
- Italy, Greece, Turkey

We manage everything from venue sourcing to cross-border vendor coordination.

**How far in advance should we start planning a destination wedding?**
We recommend starting at least 9 to 12 months in advance, especially for peak-season or international weddings. This ensures venue availability and a stress-free planning timeline.
