## WHERE ROMANCE RESTS ON LAKES

Welcome to Udaipur, the crown jewel of Rajasthan where timeless
royalty meets serene romance. Often called the City of Lakes,
Udaipur offers a magical backdrop for Destination weddings, with
its shimmering waters, majestic palaces, & sun-drenched courtyards
that breathe life into every love story.


Whether you dream of a grand celebration in a heritage palace or
an intimate lakeside wedding under the stars, Udaipur turns your
vision into a regal reality. From the iconic Jagmandir Island
Palace to the luxurious Oberoi Udaivilas, every venue in Udaipur
is steeped in elegance, history, and charm.


At Humsafar Weddings, we specialize in curating luxury destination
weddings in Udaipur, blending royal aesthetics with personal
touches that make your celebration truly unforgettable.


Because in Udaipur, your wedding isn’t just an event; it’s a royal
chapter in your love story, surrounded by beauty, blessings, and
breathtaking memories.


### WHY CHOOSE UDAIPUR FOR YOUR DESTINATION WEDDING?

- Stunning lakefront venues with heritage architecture
- Perfect blend of tradition, luxury, and scenic beauty
- Ideal for royal-themed weddings, cultural rituals, and cinematic
celebrations

- Excellent hospitality, world-class hotels, and rich Rajasthani
warmth

## Top Destination Wedding Venues in Udaipur, Rajasthan

### _THE OBEROI UDAIVILAS_

- **Rooms:** 87 rooms and suites
- **Banquets & Lawns:** 4 banquet halls accommodating
up to 600 guests; multiple lawns, including the Poolside Lawn and
Chandra Mahal

- **Distance from Airport:** ~26.4 km (approx. 49
minutes)

- **Distance from Railway Station:** ~5.8 km (approx.
21 minutes)
