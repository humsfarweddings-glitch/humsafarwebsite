Luxury Wedding Design & Décor Crafted to Reflect Your Story

At Humsafar Weddings, we believe that every wedding should tell a story — your story. Our wedding designing service is more than just beautiful décor — it’s about creating an emotional, visual, and immersive experience that reflects your personalities, cultures, and dreams.

From regal Mughal-themed mehendis to bohemian beachside mandaps, we curate every visual detail to resonate with your journey. Whether you're planning a palace wedding in Rajasthan, a beach ceremony in Goa, or an intimate celebration abroad, our designs bring soul to every celebration.

We collaborate closely with you and your family to build a design language that flows seamlessly across functions — from color palettes and floral artistry to lighting, layout, and personalized guest experiences.

Our Wedding Design Services Include:

Theme Development &amp; Creative Direction

Your story becomes our inspiration - from mood boards to design storytelling

Décor Planning &amp; Visual Styling

Floral design, set layouts, color palettes, and lighting tailored to your theme

Custom Guest Experiences &amp; Touchpoints

Personalized elements like welcome corners, story installations, and thematic entries

3D Walkthroughs, Mood Boards &amp; Design Previews

Helping you visualize your wedding before the first flower is set
