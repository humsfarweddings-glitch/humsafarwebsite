CELEBRATE AN ULTRA-LUXURY WEDDING WHERE ABU DHABI’S DESERT MEETS THE SEA

Dive into Abu Dhabi, UAE — a top international wedding destination, where opulent resorts and desert-beach fusion create a lavish romantic escape. Perfect for couples seeking the best Abu Dhabi destination wedding, this city blends luxury with Middle Eastern allure.

From top-rated venues like Emirates Palace to luxury retreats at The St. Regis Saadiyat, Abu Dhabi offers grandeur galore. Picture a beachside ceremony, a desert-inspired reception, or a romantic vow exchange in opulent halls — every moment radiates love and splendor.

At Humsafar Weddings, we craft your perfect Abu Dhab i wedding with expert planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt passion — delivering the best UAE destination wedding experience.

Because in Abu Dhabi, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s a majestic love story of desert and sea.

WHY CHOOSE ABU DHABI FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top wedding venue s: Emirates Palace, The St. Regis Saadiyat
  • Perfect blend of desert charm and luxury beach wedding vibes
  • Ideal for beach vows, grand receptions, or opulent ceremonies
  • Unique Abu Dhabi highlights: desert sunsets, luxury resorts, Middle Eastern flair
  • Best Time: November to March
  • Visa Info: Visa on Arrival (14–30 days, valid with US/UK/Schengen visa)

Top Destination Wedding Venues in Abu Dhabi, UAE

ABU DHABI, UAE

  • EMIRATES PALACE: Rooms: 394
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 2,000 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 3,000 guests Distance from Airport (Abu Dhabi International Airport): ~30 km (~35 minutes) Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable

THE ST. REGIS SAADIYAT

  • Rooms: 375
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 600 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 1,000 guests Distance from Airport (Abu Dhabi International Airport): ~20 km (~25 minutes) Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable
