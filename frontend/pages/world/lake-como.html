DESIGN AN ICONIC LAKESIDE WEDDING IN ITALY’S ENCHANTING LAKE COMO

From top-rated venues like Villa del Balbianello to luxury retreats at Villa Erba and Villa Balbiano, Lake Como blends scenic beauty with opulent style. Envision a lakeside ceremony, a grand villa reception, or a romantic vow exchange with mountain views — every moment glows with love.

At Humsafar Wedding, we craft your perfect Lake Como wedding with tailored planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt dedication — delivering the best Italy destination wedding experience.

Because in Lake Como, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s a celebrity-worthy love story framed by water and wonder.

WHY CHOOSE LAKE COMO FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top wedding venues: Villa del Balbianello, Villa Erba, Villa Balbiano
  • Perfect fusion of lakeside charm and luxury wedding elegance
  • Ideal for villa vows, grand receptions, or romantic lakeside ceremonies
  • Unique Lake Como highlights: celebrity vibes, alpine views, Italian romance
  • Best Time: April to October
  • Visa Info: Schengen Visa Required

Top Destination Wedding Venues LAKE COMO, ITALY

VILLA DEL BALBIANELLO

  • Rooms: Multiple Categories
  • Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor lawns up to 150 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Milan Malpensa Airport): ~60 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Como San Giovanni): ~25 km (~30 minutes)

VILLA ERBA

  • Rooms: Multiple Categories
  • Distance from Airport (Milan Malpensa Airport): Indoor up to 450 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 500 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Ngurah Rai International Airport): ~55 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Como San Giovanni): ~7 km (~15 minutes)

VILLA BALBIANO

  • Rooms: Multiple Categories
  • Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor lawns up to 200 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Milan Malpensa Airport): ~65 km (~1 hr 10 mins)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Como San Giovanni): ~30 km (~35 minutes)
