HOST AN INTIMATE ISLAND WEDDING ON MALDIVES’ PRISTINE BEACHES & OVERWATER VILLAS

Escape to the Maldives — India’s top international wedding destination, where private islands, overwater ceremonies, and intimate luxury create a dreamy romantic haven. Perfect for couples seeking the best Maldives destination wedding, this paradise blends seclusion with tropical bliss.

From top-rated venues like Soneva Fushi to luxury retreats at Baros and Taj Exotica, the Maldives offer unrivaled elegance. Imagine an overwater ceremony, a beachside reception, or a romantic vow exchange on a private island — every moment shines with love and serenity.

At Humsafar Weddings, we craft your perfect Maldives wedding with expert planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt passion — delivering the best international destination wedding experience.

Because in the Maldives, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s an intimate love story kissed by turquoise waves.

WHY CHOOSE MALDIVES FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top wedding venues: Soneva Fushi, Baros, Taj Exotica
  • Perfect mix of private islands and luxury wedding charm
  • Ideal for overwater vows, beach receptions, or intimate ceremonies
  • Unique Maldives highlights: turquoise lagoons, sunset cruises, tropical bliss
  • Best Time: November to April
  • Visa Info: Free Visa on Arrival (30 days)

Top Destination Wedding Venues Maldives

SONEVA FUSHI

  • Rooms: 63
  • Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor lawns up to 150 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Velana International Airport): 15 km (seaplane ~30 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable

BAROS MALDIVES

  • Rooms: 75
  • Distance from Airport (Velana International Airport): 7 km (speedboat ~25 minutes)
  • Distance from Airport (Ngurah Rai International Airport): ~55 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable

TAJ EXOTICA MALDIVES

  • Rooms: 67
  • Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor lawns up to 200 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Velana International Airport): 17 km (speedboat ~30 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable
