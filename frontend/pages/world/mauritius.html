HOST A ROMANTIC BEACH WEDDING AMIDST MAURITIUS’ CRYSTAL WATERS & LUSH LANDSCAPES

Escape to Mauritius — a top international wedding destination, where golden beaches, Indian hospitality, and multicultural backdrops create a dreamy romantic haven. Perfect for couples seeking the best Mauritius destination wedding, this island blends tropical bliss with cultural warmth.

From top-rated venues like One&Only Le Saint Géran to luxury retreats at Sugar Beach and St. Regis, Mauritius offers intimate elegance. Imagine a beachside ceremony, a grand resort reception, or a romantic vow exchange by the lagoon — every moment shines with love and beauty.

At Humsafar Weddings, we craft your perfect Mauritius wedding with expert planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt passion — delivering the best international destination wedding experience.

Because in Mauritius, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s a tropical love story of sand and soul.

WHY CHOOSE MAURITIUS FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top wedding venues: One&Only Le Saint Géran, Sugar Beach, St. Regis
  • Perfect blend of beach beauty and multicultural wedding charm
  • Ideal for beach vows, grand receptions, or intimate ceremonies
  • Unique Mauritius highlights: Indian hospitality, lagoon views, tropical allure
  • Best Time: May to December
  • Visa Info: Visa on Arrival (Free) – Up to 60 days

Top Destination Wedding Venues MAURITIUS

ONE&ONLY LE SAINT GÉRAN

  • Rooms: 112
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 300 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 400 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International): 60 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable

SUGAR BEACH MAURITIUS

  • Rooms: 186
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 250 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 300 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Ngurah Rai International Airport): ~55 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable

ST. REGIS MAURITIUS

  • Rooms: 172
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 400 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 500 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International): 55 km (~1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Not applicable
