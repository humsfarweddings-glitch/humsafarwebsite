DESIGN A RICHLY CULTURAL WEDDING ACROSS SRI LANKA’S BEACHES & TEA ESTATES

Explore Sri Lanka — a top international wedding destination, where cultural depth, golden beaches, and tea country craft a stunning romantic escape. Ideal for couples seeking the best Sri Lanka destination wedding, this island blends heritage with natural beauty.

From top-rated venues like Anantara Tangalle to luxury retreats at Shangri-La Hambantota and Amangalla, Sri Lanka offers diverse elegance. Imagine a beachside ceremony, a tea estate reception, or a romantic vow exchange with ocean views — every moment shines with love and allure.

At Humsafar Weddings, we design your ideal Sri Lanka wedding with bespoke planning, flawless execution, and heartfelt care — delivering the best international destination wedding experience.

Because in Sri Lanka, your wedding isn’t just a day — it’s a vibrant love story of culture and coast.

WHY CHOOSE SRI LANKA FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top wedding venues: Anantara Tangalle, Shangri-La Hambantota, Amangalla
  • Perfect blend of cultural depth and beach wedding charm
  • Ideal for beach vows, tea estate receptions, or intimate ceremonies
  • Unique Sri Lanka highlights: tea country views, beach sunsets, cultural rites
  • Best Time: Dec–Mar (South), May–Sept (East)
  • Visa Info: eVisa (ETA) – Easy online process (30 days)

Top Destination Wedding Venues SRI LANKA

ANANTARA TANGALLE

  • Rooms: 39
  • Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor lawns up to 150 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport): 65 km (~1.5 hours)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Hambantota): ~25 km (~45 minutes)

SHANGRI-LA HAMBANTOTA

  • Rooms: 252
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 1,000 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 2,000 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport): 20 km (~30 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Hambantota): 25 km (~45 minutes)

AMANGALLA

  • Rooms: 30
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 100 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport): 150 km (~4 hours)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Galle): 1 km (~5 minutes)
