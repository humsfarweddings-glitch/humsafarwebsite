HOST AN OPULENT WEDDING AMIDST JAIPUR’S PALACES & HERITAGE CHARM

Jaipur Step into the royal heart of Rajasthan — , a city where every corner echoes with history, heritage, and celebration. Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is the perfect destination for couples who dream of a wedding wrapped in regal elegance and rich Indian traditions.

From opulent palaces and ancient forts to vibrant gardens and cultural backdrops, Jaipur offers a stunning mix of grandeur and authenticity. Whether it's a royal wedding at Rambagh Palace, a vibrant ceremony at Fairmont Jaipur, or a traditional affair at Samode Palace, every venue tells a story of timeless romance and majestic charm.

At Humsafar Weddings, we bring your dream destination wedding in Jaipur to life with curated experiences, seamless planning, and heartfelt execution — ensuring your celebration is as grand as the love it honors.

Because in Jaipur, your wedding isn’t just an occasion — it’s a legacy of love written in royalty.

WHY CHOOSE JAIPUR FOR YOUR DESTINATION WEDDING?

  • Grand forts, royal palaces, and heritage venues
  • A perfect blend of cultural rituals and luxury hospitality
  • Ideal for traditional Indian weddings with a royal theme
  • Rich local experiences: mehendi with folk dancers, royal baraat processions, and Rajasthani cuisine

Top Destination Wedding Venues in Jaipur

Rambagh Palace – Jaipur, Rajasthan

  • Rooms: 79 luxurious rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: Multiple indoor and outdoor venues, including the Panghat Lawn; also features the SMS Convention Centre for larger gatherings
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 11 km (about 15 minutes by car)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Approximately 4.3 km (about 10 minutes by car)

Fairmont Jaipur – Jaipur, Rajasthan

  • Rooms: 245 guest rooms, including 7 suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 1 indoor banquet hall and 2 outdoor lawns
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 27.7 km (about 40 minutes by car)
  • Distance from Railway Station : Approximately 20 km (about 30 minutes by car)

JW Marriott Jaipur Resort & Spa – Jaipur, Rajasthan

  • Rooms: Approximately 200 rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: Multiple indoor and outdoor venues suitable for weddings and events
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 32 minutes by car
  • Distance from Railway Station: Approximately 30 km (about 45 minutes by car)

Hyatt Regency Jaipur Mansarovar – Jaipur, Rajasthan

  • Rooms: 245 rooms and suites, including 34 suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 4 indoor venues and 4 outdoor venues, totaling over 5,000 sq. meters of event space
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 7 km (about 15 minutes by car)
  • Distance from Railway Station: A Approximately 13 km (about 25 minutes by car)

The Leela Palace – Jaipur, Rajasthan

  • Rooms: 200 rooms, suites, and villass
  • Banquets & Lawns: 3 indoor venues and 4 outdoor venues
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 30 km (about 60 minutes by car)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Approximately 25 km (about 55 minutes by car)

Alila Fort Bishangarh – Jaipur

  • Rooms: 59 stately rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 3 meeting rooms with a total event space of 2,742 sq m, suitable for weddings and events
  • Distance from Airport: Approximately 69 km (about 1.5 hours by car)
  • Distance from Railway Station: Approximately 60 km (about 1.5 hours by car)
