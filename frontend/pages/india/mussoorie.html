CELEBRATE YOUR LOVE STORY IN MUSSOORIE’S MAJESTIC MOUNTAIN RETREAT

Discover Mussoorie, Uttarakhand — India’s premier hill station wedding destination, where misty mountains, lush valleys, and cool breezes create a magical romantic retreat. Known as the "Queen of Hills," Mussoorie is the top pick for couples seeking the best destination wedding in Uttarakhand, blending natural beauty with timeless charm.

From top-rated venues like JW Marriott Mussoorie Walnut Grove to luxury retreats such as Jaypee Residency Manor and serene settings at Savoy Mussoorie, Mussoorie fuses mountain splendor with upscale elegance. Picture a dreamy hilltop ceremony, a grand resort reception, or a romantic vow exchange with Himalayan views — every moment glows with love and wonder.

At Humsafar Weddings, we craft your ideal Mussoorie wedding with expert planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt dedication — delivering the best Uttarakhand destination wedding experience.

Because in Mussoorie, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s a breathtaking love story etched in mountains and mist.

WHY CHOOSE MUSSOORIE FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top hill station venues: JW Marriott Mussoorie, Jaypee Residency Manor, Savoy Mussoorie
  • Perfect blend of mountain serenity and luxury wedding vibes

Top Destination Wedding Venues in Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

JW MARRIOTT MUSSOORIE WALNUT GROVE RESORT & SPA

  • Rooms: 115
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 400 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 600 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Dehradun - Jolly Grant Airport): 60 km (~2 hours)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Dehradun Railway Station): 35 km (~1.5 hours)

JAYPEE RESIDENCY MANOR

  • Rooms: 135
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 300 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 500 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Dehradun - Jolly Grant Airport): 55 km (~1.5–2 hours)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Dehradun Railway Station): 33 km (~1.5 hours)
