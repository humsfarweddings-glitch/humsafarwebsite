CREATE AN EXCLUSIVE COASTAL WEDDING CELEBRATION IN ALIBAUG

Discover Alibaug, Maharashtra — India’s premier beach wedding destination, where golden shores, lush landscapes, and serene luxury craft the perfect romantic escape. Famous for its tranquil beaches and quick accessibility from Mumbai, Alibaug is the top choice for couples seeking the best destination wedding in Maharashtra for a grand yet easy getaway.

From top-rated venues like Radisson Blu to luxury retreats at Hilton Shillim and elegant settings at The Mansion House, Alibaug blends peaceful coastal charm with 5-star sophistication. Envision a stunning beach wedding ceremony, a lavish resort reception, or a romantic vow exchange by the sea — every moment shines with love and allure.

At Humsafar Weddings, we create your ideal Alibaug wedding with expert planning, seamless coordination, and heartfelt passion — delivering the best Maharashtra destination wedding experience.

Because in Alibaug, your wedding isn’t just an event — it’s a dreamy love story woven with beachside luxury and timeless magic.

WHY CHOOSE ALIBAUG FOR YOUR DREAM WEDDING?

  • Top beach wedding venues: Radisson Blu, Hilton Shillim, The Mansion House
  • Perfect fusion of serene coastal vibes and luxury wedding elegance
  • Ideal for quick Mumbai getaways, grand receptions, or intimate beach weddings
  • Unique Alibaug highlights: sunset shore strolls, fresh seafood, and easy Mumbai access

Top Destination Wedding Venues in ALIBAUG (MAHARASHTRA)

Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

  • Rooms: 156
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor: Up to 200 guests Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor: Lawns and poolside up to 600 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Mumbai – Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport): 100km by road (~3 hours), 1 hour via ferry to Mandwa Jetty + drive
  • Distance from Railway Station (Panvel Junction): 65 km (~2 hours)

Hilton Shillim Estate Retreat & Spa

  • Rooms: 99 private villas
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor: Up to 100 guests Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor: Forest deck, lawns & hilltop areas up to 300 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Pune International Airport): 55 km (~2 hours)
  • Distance from Railway Station (Lonavala Station): 25 km (~1 hour)

The Mansion House, Alibaug

  • Rooms: 25 (boutique luxury property)
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor: Lounge area for intimate events (~80–100 guests) Banquets & Lawns: Outdoor: Lawns & poolside up to 200 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Mumbai – Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport): 100km by road (~3 hours), 1 hour via ferry to Mandwa Jetty + short drive
  • Distance from Railway Station (Panvel Junction): 65 km (~2 hours)
