PLAN A MAJESTIC WEDDING IN THE HEART OF JODHPUR

Welcome to Jodhpur, where royal heritage meets desert grandeur. Known as the Blue City of Rajasthan, Jodhpur is a destination where timeless architecture, rich culture, and golden desert horizons come together to create the perfect setting for a larger-than-life wedding celebration.

Crowned by the iconic Mehrangarh Fort and surrounded by elegant palaces and sandstone havelis, Jodhpur offers a bold and regal canvas for your special day. Whether you envision a royal wedding at Umaid Bhawan Palace, a vibrant sangeet under the stars, or a traditional celebration in a heritage courtyard — Jodhpur brings your wedding dreams to life with unmatched opulence and charm.

At Humsafar Weddings, we specialize in planning luxury destination weddings in Jodhpur, blending royal aesthetics with emotional storytelling — so your wedding feels both grand and deeply personal..

WHY CHOOSE JODHPUR FOR YOUR DESTINATION WEDDING?

  • Iconic venues like Umaid Bhawan Palace, Ajit Bhawan, and Mehrangarh Fort
  • Desert charm combined with Rajput grandeur and hospitality
  • Ideal for royal-themed weddings with majestic processions and folk entertainment
  • Picture-perfect blue cityscapes and golden sunsets for stunning wedding photography

Top Destination Wedding Venues in JODHPUR

UMAID BHAWAN PALACE

  • Rooms: 70
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 180 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 750 guests
  • Distance from Airport: 5 km (~10 min)
  • Distance from Railway Station: 4 km (~8 min)

TAJ HARI MAHAL

  • Rooms: 93
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor up to 500 guests; Outdoor lawns up to 1,200 guests
  • Distance from Airport (Pantnagar Airport): 4 km (~10 min)
  • Distance from Railway Station: 2.4 km (~6 min)
