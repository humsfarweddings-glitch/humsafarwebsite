WHERE ROMANCE RESTS ON LAKES

Welcome to Udaipur, the crown jewel of Rajasthan where timeless royalty meets serene romance. Often called the City of Lakes, Udaipur offers a magical backdrop for Destination weddings, with its shimmering waters, majestic palaces, & sun-drenched courtyards that breathe life into every love story.

Whether you dream of a grand celebration in a heritage palace or an intimate lakeside wedding under the stars, Udaipur turns your vision into a regal reality. From the iconic Jagmandir Island Palace to the luxurious Oberoi Udaivilas, every venue in Udaipur is steeped in elegance, history, and charm.

At Humsafar Wedding, we specialize in curating luxury destination weddings in Udaipur, blending royal aesthetics with personal touches that make your celebration truly unforgettable.

Because in Udaipur, your wedding isn’t just an event; it’s a royal chapter in your love story, surrounded by beauty, blessings, and breathtaking memories.

WHY CHOOSE UDAIPUR FOR YOUR DESTINATION WEDDING?

  • Stunning lakefront venues with heritage architecture
  • Perfect blend of tradition, luxury, and scenic beauty
  • Ideal for royal-themed weddings, cultural rituals, and cinematic celebrations
  • Excellent hospitality, world-class hotels, and rich Rajasthani warmth

Top Destination Wedding Venues in Udaipur, Rajasthan

THE OBEROI UDAIVILAS

  • Rooms: 87 rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 4 banquet halls accommodating up to 600 guests; multiple lawns, including the Poolside Lawn and Chandra Mahal
  • Distance from Airport: ~26.4 km (approx. 49 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~5.8 km (approx. 21 minutes)

Mementos by ITC Hotels, Ekaaya

  • Rooms: 117 suites and villas
  • Banquets & Lawns: 3 meeting rooms with a total event space of 16,500 sq ft; expansive green lawns available
  • Distance from Airport: ~40.9 km (approx. 1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~28.1 km from city center

Fairmont Udaipur Palace

  • Rooms: 327 rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 3 indoor venues and 5 outdoor venues
  • Distance from Airport: ~42.8 km (approx. 45 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~12.3 km (approx. 20 minutes)

Raffles Udaipur

  • Rooms: 117 suites and villas
  • Banquets & Lawns: 3 meeting rooms with a total event space of 16,500 sq ft; expansive green lawns available
  • Distance from Airport: ~40.9 km (approx. 1 hour)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~28.1 km from city center

Taj Lake Palace

  • Rooms: 83 rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: Indoor event space for up to 75 guests in theatre-style seating; outdoor spaces available
  • Distance from Airport: ~23.5 km (approx. 45 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~10 km (approx. 10 minutes)

The Leela Palace

  • Rooms: 80 rooms and suites
  • Banquets & Lawns: 7 banquet halls accommodating up to 250 guests; additional venues include terrace, lawn, poolside, and palace spaces
  • Distance from Airport: ~28 km (approx. 45 minutes)
  • Distance from Railway Station: ~6.6 km (approx. 20 minutes)
